### About the Experiment 

In this experiment, the acidity level of milk will be determined using standard titration methods. This analysis is essential for assessing the freshness and microbial quality of milk, as increased acidity often indicates spoilage or bacterial activity. Understanding titratable acidity is crucial for monitoring milk quality during storage and processing, ensuring regulatory compliance, and maintaining product safety and consistency in dairy-based foods.
